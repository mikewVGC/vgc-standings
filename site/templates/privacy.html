Reportworm — Privacy Policy

This privacy policy ('Policy') covers the website standings.reportworm.com, ('Reportworm', 'the site').

Information Provided

Reportworm does not collect Personal Information, however it does utilize Google Analytics for the purposes of tracking general visitation trends. None of the specific information collected by Google Analytics will be shared with a third party.

Use and Disclosure of Personal Information

Reportworm will not share, rent, trade or sell your Personal Information to third parties. Personal information will be used only as described in this policy and your information will never be sold, licensed, traded or otherwise transmitted outside of the site unless express authorization is granted by you.

Updating, Correcting or Deleting Personal Information

Reportworm's data comes from different official sources, and as such things like full names will be present in the data. This includes potentially incorrect or outdated names. Reportworm makes every effort to fix data based on changes made during a specific tournament, however due to the historical nature of this data, updating names can not be done at this time.

Policy Changes

The site reserves the right to change this Policy at any time, so please review it frequently if you have any questions. The date the Policy was last modified is located at the bottom of this Policy.
